Research Group

Our group studies land-atmosphere interactions, hydrology, water resources, land surface modeling, Earth system predictability, and AI-enabled environmental prediction. We work across observations, process-based models, Earth-system models, and machine learning.

Research Areas in the Group

  • Land surface modeling and Noah-MP development
  • Hydrologic prediction and water resources
  • Droughts, floods, freezing rain, and compound extremes
  • Land-atmosphere coupling and Earth-system predictability
  • AI and machine learning for environmental prediction
  • Infrastructure resilience and climate-risk applications
